Programme, 2022

Changemaker discovery workshops

Workshops for shortlisted applicants in two districts, the first step into the twelve-month programme.

When
18 October 2022
Where
Batticaloa and Ampara
Format
Discovery workshops

DreamSpace Academy ran changemaker discovery workshops in October 2022 for shortlisted applicants to the third cohort under Project AISLE: at the DreamSpace Hive for applicants from Batticaloa, and at the Sarvodaya District Centre for applicants from Ampara.

Discovery is the first of the programme’s three stages. A person who comes through it is a changemaker candidate: they have shown they are motivated, but they have not built anything yet.

A candidate becomes a changemaker at the next stage. To get there, they produce a solution to a problem raised by the community. A real user who is not on the project team has to test it, and someone has to depend on it.
