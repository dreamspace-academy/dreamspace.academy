How to read the numbers on these pages

Where a page gives a number of participants, it is the number of people in the room on that one day. Each number is for one session, so the numbers are never added up into a total.

A repeated title means the same workshop or course run again for a different group. Attendance at large public events is kept apart from workshop attendance.

What is missing from these pages

The list runs from the founding in September 2018 to today and is not complete. A quiet month may simply be a month when nothing was written down.

Some pages give only a date and a place, because that is all that is known. They are left short rather than filled out with guesses, and they are the ones most likely to grow later.

Some years are much fuller than others. 2021 has few workshops but a launch to the International Space Station; 2024 is one of the fullest years. Those years saw a pandemic, an economic crisis and a recovery, and the pages show them as they were.
