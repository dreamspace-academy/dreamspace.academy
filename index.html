DreamSpace Academy

Co-Creating The Dreams

A non-profit social enterprise in Batticaloa, Sri Lanka. Since 2018 it has helped young people and communities learn, make, and turn ideas into work that solves local problems.

How learners grow

The Changemaker Journey

The Changemaker Journey describes how learners in the STEAM programmes grow, from trying things out for the first time to making a difference in a community. Each stage names what the work is for.

How far someone has gone in one subject is tracked separately, by the levels of a course. A level belongs to the course, never to the person.

  1. Explorer

    “What can I do?”

    Try tools and ideas for the first time, with someone there to guide.

  2. Maker

    “Can I make something?”

    Build solid skill in one chosen subject through longer projects that are assessed.

  3. Innovator

    “Can I make it better?”

    Use those skills on a real problem from outside the classroom, one with no set answer.

  4. Changemaker

    “Can I make a difference?”

    Turn work that has been tested into a lasting contribution to a community.

The STEAM programmes are one part of what DreamSpace Academy teaches. The academy also runs business and entrepreneurship training, digital security and media literacy training, and training for teachers and people already at work. What the academy teaches.

Empowerment Inside School

Practical ICT, robotics and STEAM learning inside government schools, from Grade 6 to Grade 11. It began in Batticaloa in July 2025 and now runs in Batticaloa, Kantale and Hatton.

About the programme

The MakerSpace

MakerSpace by DreamSpace Academy

MakerSpace by DreamSpace Academy is a community makerspace that local partners can set up in their own area, as a social franchise. The local partner provides the space. DreamSpace Academy provides the design of the unit, the brand, the operating standard (the rules it is run by) and a licence to run the academy's programmes there.

The programmes do not need a MakerSpace. They also run in schools, at partner sites and online.

The purpose

To put the capability to solve local challenges into local hands, and the conditions that keep it there.

Mission

Co-creating with underserved communities the capability to keep solving their own challenges, through education, innovation and entrepreneurship.

Vision

Communities solving their own challenges.

The goal: one hundred local challenges solved by local people, by 2050. Progress will be measured against a published definition of a solved local challenge, not by the number of workshops run.
