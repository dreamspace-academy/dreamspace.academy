Each entry links to the publisher, the DOI record (a permanent link for an academic paper) or the official project page where one exists. A few entries, mostly books and reports, have no stable public link, so they are listed with enough detail to find in a library catalogue.

What a citation here does and does not mean

These references show the published research the approach is built on. They do not mean that any author or institution listed supports DreamSpace Academy, has reviewed this site, or agrees with how their work has been used. Where a page takes a finding further than its authors did, that page says so.

Corrections

When a citation, figure or link on this site is corrected, the page’s updated date changes.

If you find a reference that names the wrong author, has been replaced by a newer edition, or is used to say something it does not say, please tell DreamSpace Academy.

Two mistakes are checked for most closely: a statistic that a newer edition of the same national bulletin has replaced, and a finding quoted as stronger than its authors claimed.
