Definitional reviews

27 reviews of the terms this work is built on. Each one looks at how its term is defined in published research, where the sources agree and where they differ, and ends with its own numbered list of sources.

Several of these words are used loosely, and two of them have no official definition at all. That matters whenever a number is counted against one of them. Short definitions are in the glossary. These reviews are the long versions.

Pedagogy and delivery

  • Assessment in Maker Education A definitional review of how learning in maker settings is assessed, process versus product evidence, portfolios, self- and peer-assessment, rubrics and their limits, and the interview method for eliciting what a learner understands about an artifact they built.
  • Challenge-Based Learning A definitional review of Challenge-Based Learning, its lineage, its framework, and the single feature that distinguishes it from project- and problem-based learning.
  • Direct and Explicit Instruction A definitional review separating Direct Instruction (a specific family of scripted, field-tested curricula) from direct and explicit instruction as a general teacher-led mode, and reporting the unsettled dispute over how much guidance a novice learner needs.
  • Maker Education A definitional review of maker education, its lineage, what the systematic reviews actually report as outcomes, and the persistent gap between its learning claims and its evidence.
  • Open-Ended and Self-Directed Making A definitional review of unstructured, self-directed making time as a distinct pedagogical mode, the names it goes by, the benefits claimed for it, the well-documented equity critique, and the finding that open and structured modes are complementary.
  • Problem-Based Learning A definitional review of Problem-Based Learning, the medical-education pedagogy in which an ill-structured problem starts the learning, and the reason it shares an acronym with a pedagogy it is not a variant of.
  • Project-Based Learning A definitional review of Project-Based Learning, the five criteria that screen a project in, the progressive-era lineage, and the acronym it shares with an unrelated pedagogy from medical education.
  • Tinkering A definitional review of tinkering, the Exploratorium tradition, its constructionist lineage, what "low floor, wide walls, high ceiling" actually specifies, and why the literature does not show that tinkering alone produces conceptual learning.

Innovation and enterprise

  • Changemaker A definitional review of "changemaker", a movement term coined at Ashoka rather than an academic construct, deliberately ungated by design, and therefore uncountable.
  • Entrepreneur A definitional review of the term entrepreneur, the fifteen elements that recur across two centuries of definitions, the six a leading review distils from them, and why the field declines to settle on one.
  • Entrepreneurial Ecosystem A definitional review of the entrepreneurial ecosystem, a concept that reached policy ubiquity in under a decade while its own leading proponents were describing it as tautological, under-theorised and long on component lists but short on causal mechanism.
  • Entrepreneurship Education A definitional review of entrepreneurship education, the about/for/through distinction, the intention-as-proxy problem, and an evidence base whose effects are small, dosage-dependent, and occasionally negative.
  • Grassroots Innovation A definitional review of grassroots innovation, the two research strands that developed the term independently, the social-economy criterion that distinguishes it from market innovation, and the unresolved question of whether an aided innovation still counts.
  • Impact Venture A definitional review of what to call a mission-driven trading organisation, and the finding that "impact venture" has no sector standard, only one institutional definition, which places it by financing stage rather than by values.
  • Innovation A definitional review of innovation, a term with no authoritative definition, a literature that says so openly, and the one distinction every source nonetheless agrees on, namely that an idea becomes an innovation only when it is adopted and used.
  • Social Enterprise A definitional review of social enterprise, a term with four competing definitions, no settled boundary, and a legal-form question that is separate from it in every jurisdiction.
  • Startup A definitional review of the term startup, why the literature has settled on no single definition, and which variables actually do the discriminating work.

Purpose and community

  • Co-creation A definitional review of co-creation across four traditions that use the word differently, and the feature that separates it from consultation, participation and collaboration.
  • Underserved Communities A definitional review of "underserved", a relational claim about inadequate provision of a named service, distinct from marginalised, vulnerable and disadvantaged, and contested for locating deficit in the community rather than in the systems that failed it.

Learning theory

  • Cognitive Apprenticeship A definitional review of cognitive apprenticeship, the premise that trade apprenticeship makes process visible while schooling leaves thinking invisible, the six teaching methods and four design dimensions that follow from it, and what the evidence base does and does not support.
  • Communities of Practice A definitional review of communities of practice, the concept's origin as an analytic of how learning happens in practice, its later reinvention as a prescriptive management tool, and the critiques of power and harmony that follow from both.
  • Constructionism A definitional review of Papert's constructionism, what it adds to Piaget's constructivism, why its author refused to define it, and whether maker education actually implements it.
  • Experiential Learning A definitional review of experiential learning, Kolb's synthesis of Dewey, Lewin and Piaget, the four-stage cycle and its two dialectics, and why the cycle and the Learning Style Inventory rest on entirely different evidence.

The space and its sector

  • Makerspace A definitional review of the makerspace, why the term resists definition, what actually separates a makerspace from a hackerspace, a FabLab, a TechShop and a community workshop, and how thin the outcome evidence still is.
  • Organisational Lifecycle A definitional review of the organisational lifecycle, a descriptive family of stage models whose biological metaphor imports assumptions about ageing, decline and irreversibility that the empirical evidence does not support.
  • Social Franchise A definitional review of social franchising, replication of a proven social model through a franchise-like agreement, the spectrum it sits on, and the quality-control-versus-local-adaptation tension that is the field's central unresolved problem.
  • Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) A definitional review of Technical and Vocational Education and Training, the term, its rival names, the formal/informal split, the record of dual-system transfer, and an evidence base whose measured employment effects are far smaller and less durable than the policy rhetoric implies.
